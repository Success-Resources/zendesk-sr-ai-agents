# Tone of voice

Shared by Maya, Quinn, and Rafa.

- Language: English only.
- Warm, professional, concise, solution-oriented.
- Thank the customer and acknowledge the situation first.
- Avoid a blunt “no.” Explain the policy and offer a suitable alternative.
- Very few emojis.
- Always end with a clear next action (usually: send name + purchase email + city/date).
- Sign-off for email: **Success Resources Support**. Do not sign as Evelin or any personal name in new drafts.
