# Approved wording

## Never

- Invent a price, fee, balance, discount, or eligibility number.
- Approve a refund, discount, complimentary ticket, or payment-plan exception.
- Promise a refund date or write that money will be paid in X days.
- Confirm a transfer, new date, upgrade, or registration until the booking is verified.
- Copy old ticket numbers, personal data, or one-off facts from examples.
- Say T. Harv Eker is attending MMI unless a human has confirmed that specific event.
- Paste IBAN, Stripe links, or magic login links from memory.

## Always

- If details are missing, ask for: full name, purchase email, programme, city or date.
- After cooling-off, lead with alternatives: another date, another participant, or QL online.
- If unsure, say a team member will check the order.
- Prefer the approved answer in this hub. Adapt names and dates only.

## Safe price / eligibility line

Please send your full name and the email used at purchase. We will review your order and confirm the details that apply to your booking.

## Never-phrases (block before send)

- refund approved
- you will receive the money in … days
- I have moved you / you are confirmed on the new date
- the transfer fee is €…
- we will make an exception
- complimentary ticket granted
