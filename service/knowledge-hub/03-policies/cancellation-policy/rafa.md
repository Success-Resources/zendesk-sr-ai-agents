# Cancellation policy — Rafa

- Customer wants to cancel after cooling-off: treat as a refund/change request. Alternatives first (see refund policy). Do not approve.
- Medical reason: empathise, collect purchase email, programme/date, and medical documentation. Management reviews. No promise.
- Company cancels or postpones: acknowledge. Team writes with the next step. Do not invent compensation.
- T&Cs: company date change is not a refund trigger; force majeure cancel by the company can refund monies paid — escalate that case.
