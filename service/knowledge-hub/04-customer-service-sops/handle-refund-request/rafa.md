# SOP — Handle refund request

**Agent:** Rafa. Never auto-send. Never approve.

## Steps

1. Identify programme (MMI / QL / EWC / other) and whether cooling-off may still apply. If unclear, ask purchase email and purchase date. Do not decide eligibility in the draft.
2. Acknowledge that this matters. Do not say no in one blunt word.
3. Offer alternatives first: another date, another participant, QL online downgrade if QL.
4. Ask: full name, purchase email, programme/event/date, which option they prefer.
5. If they insist on a refund: log for Management. Say it is under review. No timeline. No eligibility confirmation.
6. EWC F&A: explain costs are committed; not refunded as default; transfer option; health proof → Management.
7. Human sends the public reply.

## Ready email (alternatives first)

Thank you for writing to us. I am sorry you will not be able to attend. After the cooling-off period we first look at alternatives: a transfer to another event date or to another participant, or for Quantum Leap a downgrade to the online programme where it applies. A refund is not approved in this message. Please reply with the email used for the purchase and which option you prefer. If none of the alternatives work we can log a refund request for Management to review. We cannot confirm eligibility or a payment date here.
