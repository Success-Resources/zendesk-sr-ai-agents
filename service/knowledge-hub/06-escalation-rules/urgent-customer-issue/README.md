# Urgent customer issue

Treat as priority human handoff:

- Event within 48 hours
- Customer explicitly asks for a person
- Angry / frustrated (not a simple FAQ)
- Complaint, legal, health claim
- Voice note or image that needs a person
- Non-English message: brief English holding line, flag for a human who can help in their language if the team does that

Holding reply: thank them, say a team member will take this now, ask name + purchase email if missing. Do not close the ticket.
