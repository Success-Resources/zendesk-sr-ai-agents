# Finance escalation

## When to escalate

- Refund request rejected and customer disputes the decision
- Customer claims double charge, missing payment, or payment taken but no registration
- Customer demands a refund exception outside cooling-off
- Any promise or decision that changes money owed

## What Maya should do

1. Acknowledge the concern
2. Collect registration email, event, purchase date, and payment details the customer can type as text
3. State that a team member will review
4. Do **not** approve, deny, or invent refund amounts

## Approved wording

- Rejected refund: entry **5.5**
- Frustrated / complaint: entries **8.1**, **8.5**
- Ask for human: entry **8.4**
