# Management escalation

## When to escalate

- Formal complaint that needs ownership beyond frontline CS
- Reputation / legal / media risk language from the customer
- Repeated unresolved complaint after finance or event review
- Customer explicitly asks for a manager or “someone senior”

## What Maya should do

1. Stay calm and respectful
2. Collect a clear written summary of the issue and desired outcome
3. Escalate — do not negotiate settlements
4. Use complaint / human-request templates (**8.4**, **8.5**)
