# SOP — Handle refund request

## Goal

Guide the customer through the refund process without promising an outcome Maya cannot guarantee.

## Steps

1. **Identify** purchase email, event/city, and purchase date if shared.
2. **Explain cooling-off:**
   - Europe: 14 working days from purchase
   - Asia: 5 working days from purchase
3. **Share the form:** https://sr-event.typeform.com/to/ccysEJGM
4. **Ask the customer to reply** after submitting so the case can be tracked.
5. **Set expectation:** approved refunds typically take 7–14 working days once processed.
6. **If outside cooling-off:** explain refunds are generally unavailable; offer person/event transfer instead.
7. **If rejected / disputed / angry:** escalate to finance (see escalation rules). Do not argue policy in a confrontational way.
