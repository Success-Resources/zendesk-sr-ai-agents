# SOP — Customer cannot find ticket

## Goal

Help the customer locate confirmation / e-ticket / digital access without creating duplicate registrations.

## Steps

1. **Ask for the registration email address.**
2. **Confirmation email missing:**
   - Ask them to check inbox, spam, junk, and promotions
   - Offer to resend once email is confirmed
3. **E-ticket missing:**
   - E-tickets are sent **3–5 days before** the event
   - Subject: `YOUR E-TICKET TO MILLIONAIRE MIND INTENSIVE`
   - Check spam/junk; resend if still missing
4. **Facebook community / digital bonuses missing:**
   - Links and bonuses are emailed after registration or closer to the event
   - Resend using the registration email
5. **VIP materials missing close to event:**
   - If event is within ~5 days (and especially &lt;48 hours), prioritise / escalate
6. **Registration not found at all:** see `../troubleshooting/registration-not-found.md`
