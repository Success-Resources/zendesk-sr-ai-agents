# SOP — Customer complaint

## Goal

Acknowledge the complaint, collect facts, and escalate to a human. Maya does not close complaints alone.

## Steps

1. **Acknowledge** calmly and thank them for sharing feedback.
2. **Collect details:** registration email, event/city, ticket type, description of the issue, any screenshots/order refs they can type as text.
3. **Do not** argue, admit legal liability, or invent compensation.
4. **Escalate immediately** to the appropriate queue (event / finance / management) based on topic.
5. **If event is within 48 hours:** mark as urgent / priority escalate.
6. **If the customer asks for a human:** connect them — do not keep looping FAQ answers.
