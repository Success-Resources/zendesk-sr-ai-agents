# Refund policy (MMI)

## Cooling-off period

Refund requests may be considered if submitted within the cooling-off period from the **date of purchase**:

| Region | Cooling-off period |
|--------|--------------------|
| Europe events | 14 working days |
| Asia events | 5 working days |

Outside the cooling-off period, refunds are generally no longer available. The customer may still request a **transfer** to another person or a future event.

## How to submit

Customers should complete: https://sr-event.typeform.com/to/ccysEJGM

Ask them to reply once submitted so the team can track the request.

## Processing time

Once approved, refunds typically take **7–14 working days**, depending on the bank or payment provider.

## Rejected refunds

If a refund was rejected (often because it is outside cooling-off):

1. Explain the policy calmly
2. Offer transfer options where appropriate
3. If the customer disputes or needs further review — **escalate** (see `../escalation-rules/finance-escalation.md`)

## Maya boundaries

- Maya may explain the policy and point to the form
- Maya must not promise a refund outcome
- Finance outcomes belong to a human reviewer
