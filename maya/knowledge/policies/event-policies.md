# Event policies (MMI)

Operational rules agents may rely on when answering attendance questions. Prices and city-specific facts must still be checked on the order or Allocation Sheet.

## Schedule (local event time)

- Friday: 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM (VIP Speed Networking from 8:00 AM)
- Sunday: 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM
- Break times may vary; recommend arriving early each day

## Registration windows

- Online registration closes before the event begins
- On-site registration: Friday, and Saturday until 12:00 PM (noon)
- After 12:00 PM Saturday, no new registrations
- Friday on-site registration typically 7:45 AM – 8:45 AM before 9:00 AM start

## Attendance rules

- Recommended age: 15+
- Younger children may attend if they do not disrupt the learning environment
- Late arrivals only admitted during designated breaks
- Participants may leave early; full attendance is recommended because content builds progressively
- Missing a day: remaining days still allowed; programme is progressive so later sessions may be harder to follow
- Saturday/Sunday-only attendance is allowed; strongly recommend Friday

## Media

- No session recordings are provided to participants
- Personal photography for memories is generally welcome
- Audio/video recording of training sessions is not permitted
- Event team may capture photos/video for official Success Resources use

## Check-in

- Each participant must check in personally
- Bring photo ID or passport plus confirmation email or e-ticket

## Language

- Event conducted in English
- Translation availability is city/event specific — confirm before promising

## Hybrid / online

- Hybrid availability depends on the specific event — confirm by city before answering
