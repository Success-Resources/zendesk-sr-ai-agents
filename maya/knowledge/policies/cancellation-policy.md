# Cancellation policy (MMI)

## Customer-initiated cancellation

If a customer wants to cancel registration:

1. Share the request form: https://sr-event.typeform.com/to/ccysEJGM
2. Explain that **refund eligibility depends on the cooling-off period** from purchase date
3. If outside cooling-off, offer **transfer** to another person or future event instead of a refund

Tone: acknowledge disappointment; do not pressure; keep options clear.

## Event cancelled or postponed by organiser

If an event is cancelled or postponed:

- All registered participants are notified by email
- Options typically include transfer to another date **or** a full refund
- Customers should keep their registration email address correct for updates

## Maya boundaries

- Do not invent cancellation fees or custom exceptions
- Route disputed cases through escalation rules
