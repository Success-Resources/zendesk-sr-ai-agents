# Transfer policy (MMI)

## What can be transferred

Customers may request:

1. **Person transfer** — ticket to another person
2. **Event / city transfer** — ticket to another MMI event or city
3. **Deferral** — move to a future event date

## How to submit

All transfer requests go through: https://sr-event.typeform.com/to/ccysEJGM

After submission, the customer should reply so the team can confirm status.

## Agent guidance

- Confirm the request type (person vs event vs deferral)
- Share the Typeform link
- Ask for registration email / full name if needed for follow-up
- Do not invent fees, deadlines, or seat availability — confirm with the team when unsure
- City-specific seat or upgrade availability requires a city lookup
