# Registration not found

## Symptoms

- Customer says they paid but have no confirmation
- Email not recognised when they try to get help
- Name does not match any registration the team can see

## Handling

1. Ask for: full name, email used at checkout, approximate purchase date, event city, last 4 digits / payment method if they can share
2. Ask them to check spam/junk for confirmation from the registration flow
3. If still missing — escalate to registration/finance to locate the order
4. Do **not** create a new paid registration assumption or promise a free seat
