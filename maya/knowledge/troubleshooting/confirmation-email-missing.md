# Confirmation email missing

## Standard script

1. Confirmation email has been sent for completed registrations
2. Ask customer to check **inbox, spam, junk, and promotions**
3. If still missing, ask for the **email address used during registration**
4. Resend / human follow-up once email is confirmed

## E-ticket timing

- E-tickets are sent **3–5 days before** the event
- Subject line: `YOUR E-TICKET TO MILLIONAIRE MIND INTENSIVE`
- Same spam-folder checks apply

## Approved wording

- Confirmation: entry **4.5**
- E-ticket: entry **4.6**
- Facebook group link in confirmation: entry **4.9**
- Digital bonuses: entry **4.11**
