## 4.2 — When does registration close?
**Category:** Registration & Ticketing Admin | **Tag:** Universal
### WhatsApp
Hello! Online registration closes before the event. On-site registration is available on Friday and on Saturday until 12pm (noon).

We recommend registering early to secure your preferred ticket type and any early bird pricing.
### Email
Hello,

Thank you for your question.

Online registration closes before the event begins.

If you wish to register on-site, registration is available on Friday and Saturday until 12:00 PM (noon). After 12:00 PM on Saturday, no new registrations will be accepted.

We recommend registering early to secure your preferred ticket type and any available promotional pricing.

Best regards,
---
