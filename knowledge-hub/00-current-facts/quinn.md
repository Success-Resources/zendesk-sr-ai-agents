## Can my QL programme or NWA ticket be transferred

QL package programmes are personal allocations. They cannot be exchanged, changed to a different programme, or transferred to another person. Promotional or heavily reduced NWA tickets are not transferable to another person or date; a la carte NWA purchases may be, after the team checks. TTT or NWA postponement for personal reasons is usually accepted and moved to the next year, after the team confirms. An EWC date change close to the start needs a medical certificate.

## How long are QL redemption codes valid

QL redemption codes are valid for one calendar year. New codes for the next year are issued in December. Redemption uses https://sr-event.com/ql-redemption with the code from the QL bonuses email; buying on the official event page is not the same as redeeming.

## Is there Polish or Spanish translation at NWA

## How is a QL contract cancelled

Staff only, never in the customer email: the team requires a picture of the destroyed QL badge before the contract is cancelled, and may first offer a short call. Refund and cancellation terms are for the team to state, not the draft.
