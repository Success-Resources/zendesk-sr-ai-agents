# SOP — Handle refund request

**Agent:** Rafa. Never auto-send. Never approve.

## Steps

1. Identify programme (MMI / QL / EWC / other) and whether cooling-off may still apply. If unclear, ask purchase email and purchase date. Do not decide eligibility in the draft.
2. Acknowledge that this matters. Do not say no in one blunt word.
3. Do not offer a transfer, a refund, or a Quantum Leap downgrade until the event and booking are identified. Do not assume the cooling-off period has passed.
4. Ask: full name, purchase email, programme/event/date, which option they prefer.
5. If they insist on a refund: log for Management. Say it is under review. No timeline. No eligibility confirmation.
6. EWC F&A: explain costs are committed; not refunded as default; transfer option; health proof → Management.
7. Human sends the public reply.

## Ready email (alternatives first)

Thank you for writing to us. I am sorry you will not be able to attend. Please send the event name and date, the email used for the purchase, and your order or ticket number if they are not already in the thread. Once the booking is located, we can check the cancellation terms, including a cooling-off window where the contract provides one. A refund is not approved in this message. If a refund is approved, processing is up to 45 working days. We cannot confirm eligibility or a payment date here.
