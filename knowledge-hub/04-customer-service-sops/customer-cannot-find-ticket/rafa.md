# SOP — Customer cannot find ticket

**Agents:** Maya (MMI e-ticket / confirmation), Quinn (QL event confirmation), Rafa if it is actually a payment/invoice issue.

## Steps

1. Apologise. Do not invent the venue.
2. Ask: full name as registered, purchase email, programme / city / date, approximate purchase date if known.
3. Team locates the booking and resends to the email on the order.
4. Ask them to check spam / junk / promotions.
5. MMI e-ticket is often sent 3–5 days before the event (subject line may say YOUR E-TICKET TO MILLIONAIRE MIND INTENSIVE). Confirm current practice before stating a number of days if unsure.
