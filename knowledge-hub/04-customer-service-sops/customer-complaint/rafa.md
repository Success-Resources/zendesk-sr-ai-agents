# SOP — Customer complaint

**Escalate.** This is not an FAQ.

## Steps

1. Thank them. Acknowledge the frustration in one line. Do not defend the company at length.
2. Collect: name, purchase email, programme, what went wrong, what they want.
3. Do not offer a refund, discount, or complimentary ticket to “make it right.”
4. Route: complaints and special payment arrangements → Evelin. Legal / refund approval / discounts → Management.
5. Short holding reply only. A human owns the case.
6. Event within 48 hours or health/legal: mark urgent.
