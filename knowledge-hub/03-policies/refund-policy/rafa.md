# Refund policy — Rafa

**Owner agent:** Rafa. Drafts only. A person always sends. **Never write that a refund is approved.**

## Operational policy (Europe CS)

Do not assume the 14-day window has passed. Check the purchase date against the contract. Do not offer a transfer, a refund, or a Quantum Leap downgrade until the booking is identified.

Any refund consideration must be reviewed by Management. Do not promise a refund. If a refund is approved, processing is up to 45 working days.

**Internal only (never tell the customer):** approved refunds are processed staff → venues → facilitators → participants last. Never promise a refund date.

## EWC food & accommodation

Not refunded as the default. Costs are committed in advance. Health-related: collect medical proof, then Management decides.

## Published T&Cs (online purchase)

Orders are binding. Do not say there is no cooling-off period. Check the purchase date against the cancellation terms. Where the contract provides a 14-day window, verify it. Do not assume it, and do not deny it. If the terms and the Europe practice differ, escalate. Do not invent which rule wins.

## Phrases that must never appear

- refund approved
- you will receive the money in … days
- we will make an exception
- complimentary ticket granted
